01 / THE FORM

Five, then three.

The eight-bar loop divides into five bars of statement and three bars of response. Bar six marks the golden-section turn. Repetition is intentional; change has a purpose.

02 / THE RHYTHM

Evenly uneven.

Euclidean rhythms distribute accents over a fixed grid. Conventional anchors keep the beat grounded; controlled variation gives it movement.

03 / THE AI

Rules, not prompts.

A symbolic composition engine chooses harmony, builds a motif and transforms it. Identical seed and settings produce identical note events.
