Arrangement

Choose sounds, mute or solo tracks, and regenerate individual parts with ↻. Use Sound library to import your own samples.

Genre and subgenre shape the actual rhythms, melody and instrument palette. The sixth bar marks the golden-section change. Scroll sideways to see later bars on smaller screens.

Harmony

Change any chord to rebuild the pitched parts around it. Changing key or mode starts a new progression.

How the composition is generated
Sample sources: TR-808 / Michael Fischer and Meta Open Sound, Octal house-core (CC0) and tonejs-instruments (CC BY 3.0), VCSL (CC0), and Vocobox Human Voice Dataset (MIT, vocals by Martin). Imported audio stays on your device.

Sound library

Import a recording once, then use it on any channel.

WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC and other browser-supported formats. Maximum 25 MB per file and 150 MB for the local library. Audio is saved in your browser where storage is supported, never uploaded. Share links can't carry the actual files.
